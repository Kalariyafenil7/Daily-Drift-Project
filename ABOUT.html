About Daily Drift

We're on a mission to make bike pooling accessible, sustainable, and community-driven. Join us in creating a better way to commute.

Our Story

Daily Drift was born from a simple observation: thousands of people bike the same routes every day, often alone.

What started as a small project among friends has grown into a platform serving riders across the city.

Today, we're proud to be part of the growing movement toward sustainable urban transportation, one ride at a time.
